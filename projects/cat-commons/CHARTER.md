# Project Charter - CAT Commons

## Context & Motivation

CAT preparation resources currently live across personal Drive folders, old spreadsheets, public links, and community memory. Aspirants repeatedly need the same orientation, while each cohort risks inheriting stale links, unclear ownership, and material that should not be redistributed.

CAT Commons exists to provide a durable, ethical starting point: Krishna Chaudhari's *HowToACE - CAT 1.1* guide first, followed by a compact set of current, rights-cleared resources and a human community that can maintain the path for future cohorts.

## Scope

### In scope

- Maintain one canonical, Foundation-governed CAT resource folder.
- Publish the latest author-approved *HowToACE - CAT* guide as the start-here document.
- Curate current public links, official resources, legitimately distributable notes, PYQs, planning templates, and mentorship entry points.
- Record source, owner, year, redistribution status, and review date for every public artifact.
- Preserve a clear contributor and cohort-steward handoff.
- Verify the aspirant journey before each new cohort.

### Out of scope

- Republishing commercial coaching videos, paid mock content, torrents, or other material without written redistribution rights.
- Storing learner email, application records, scores, identity documents, or private conversations.
- Promising admissions outcomes, percentile guarantees, or personalized admissions advice.
- Treating GitHub as storage for large files, backups, credentials, or private exports.
- Replacing coaching, peer support, or the aspirant's own judgment.

## Retirement Condition

CAT Commons retires when either:

1. its maintained, rights-cleared resources and stewardship are absorbed into another JSF education cell; or
2. no active cohort or maintainer has used or reviewed it for 24 consecutive months.

Retirement requires a final public export of the rights-cleared index, removal of stale public links, and a recorded disposition for the Drive folder. Private or unlicensed material is not exported into JSF.

## Governance Inheritance

This project inherits JSF root:

- `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` - no override
- `CONTRIBUTING.md` - no override
- `LICENSE` (Apache-2.0) - applies to repository-authored documentation, not third-party resources

Any override requires founding cell approval and an entry in `governance/DECISIONS.md`.
